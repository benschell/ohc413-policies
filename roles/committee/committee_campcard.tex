\section{Role Overview}
The Camp Card Coordinator runs the troop's Camp Card sale each Spring, distributing cards to Scouts and coordinating with the council and Treasurer so proceeds go toward summer camp costs.

\vspace{0.4em}
\section{Responsibilities}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Order camp cards from the council for the troop's participating Scouts
  \item Schedule storefront sale locations and times, and coordinate with the Secretary to get dates on the troop calendar
  \item Coordinate with the Treasurer to ensure change and card payment processing are available at storefront sales
  \item Promote the sale and any troop incentives to families
  \item Issue cards to Scouts per Troop 413 Policies \& Procedures
  \item Coordinate with the Treasurer to credit sales proceeds to individual Scout accounts
  \item Return unsold cards and settle final payment with the council by the deadline
\end{itemize}

\section{Training Requirements}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Required:} Current Safeguarding Youth Training (SYT)
  \item \textbf{Recommended:} Attend the council's Camp Card kickoff/training session
\end{itemize}

\section{Notes \& Resources}
Camp Card program details, pricing, and ordering deadlines are published by the council each year. The outgoing Coordinator should hand off the council contact, current card inventory count, and the per-Scout sales tracking sheet during transition. This is a functional role: a registered committee member is preferred, but it may be filled by program leadership if needed.
