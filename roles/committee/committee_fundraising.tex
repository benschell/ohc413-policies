\section{Role Overview}
The Fundraising Chair plans and oversees the troop's fundraising calendar, coordinating the Popcorn Kernel and Camp Card Coordinator on their council-sponsored sales and working with the Treasurer so proceeds are credited to the right Scout accounts.

\vspace{0.4em}
\section{Responsibilities}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Plan the troop's annual fundraising calendar (popcorn in Fall, camp cards in Spring)
  \item Recruit and support the Popcorn Kernel and Camp Card Coordinator
  \item Coordinate with the Treasurer to credit fundraising proceeds to individual Scout accounts
  \item Communicate fundraising opportunities, deadlines, and incentives to families
  \item Organize any additional troop-specific fundraisers as needed
  \item Track overall fundraising participation and results
\end{itemize}

\section{Training Requirements}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Required:} Current Safeguarding Youth Training (SYT)
  \item \textbf{Required:} Scouts BSA - Troop Committee Position-Specific Training (my.scouting.org)
\end{itemize}
