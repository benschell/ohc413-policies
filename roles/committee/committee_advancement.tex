\section{Role Overview}
The Advancement Coordinator coordinates rank advancement (Scoutbook records, Boards of Review, and badge presentation) so Scouts are recognized accurately and promptly for their progress. This role works closely with the Scoutmaster to keep advancement fair, consistent, and on schedule.

\vspace{0.4em}
\section{Responsibilities}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Maintain accurate advancement records in Scoutbook, working with the Troop Scribe
  \item Schedule Boards of Review and recruit committee members to serve on them
  \item Order rank badges and awards, ensuring timely presentation at Courts of Honor
  \item Maintain the troop's merit badge counselor list
  \item Coordinate with the Scoutmaster on individual Scout advancement progress
  \item Communicate advancement requirements and opportunities to families
  \item Support Eagle Scout candidates through the Eagle board of review process
\end{itemize}

\section{Training Requirements}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Required:} Current Safeguarding Youth Training (SYT)
  \item \textbf{Required:} Scouts BSA - Troop Committee Position-Specific Training (my.scouting.org)
\end{itemize}

\section{Expectations}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Attend monthly committee meetings
  \item Be familiar with and follow Troop 413 Policies \& Procedures
  \item Complete assigned tasks by agreed deadlines
  \item Attend quarterly Courts of Honor in the Field Uniform
  \item Ensure Boards of Review are scheduled promptly so Scouts aren't left waiting
  \item Model Scout values in all interactions
  \item Coordinate with outgoing role-holder during transition

\section{Notes \& Resources}
Keep the Scoutbook admin access list and merit badge counselor roster in a shared location. Key BSA resources: \textit{Guide to Advancement} (BSA \#33088) and \textit{Troop Committee Guidebook} (BSA \#34505). The outgoing Advancement Coordinator should hand off Scoutbook admin access, the current Board of Review schedule, and the merit badge counselor list during transition. This is a required registered committee position; it cannot be filled by program leadership or an unregistered volunteer.
