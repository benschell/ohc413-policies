\section{Role Overview}
Assistant Scoutmasters support the Scoutmaster in delivering the troop's program, mentoring youth leadership and providing supervision at meetings and outings. ASMs often take direct responsibility for a specific patrol or program area under the Scoutmaster's direction.

\vspace{0.3em}
\section{Responsibilities}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Support the Scoutmaster at troop meetings, outings, and events
  \item Mentor and coach youth leaders in an assigned patrol or program area
  \item Provide two-deep adult leadership coverage at events per Youth Protection guidelines
  \item Conduct Scoutmaster Conferences for rank advancement when delegated by the Scoutmaster
  \item Communicate with the Scoutmaster on Scout progress and concerns
\end{itemize}

\section{Training Requirements}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Required:} Current Safeguarding Youth Training (SYT)
  \item \textbf{Required:} Scoutmaster/Assistant Scoutmaster Leader Specific Training (my.scouting.org)
  \item \textbf{Required:} Introduction to Outdoor Leader Skills (IOLS)
  \item \textbf{Required:} Hazardous Weather Training (my.scouting.org)
  \item \textbf{Recommended:} Wood Badge
\end{itemize}

\section{Expectations}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Attend troop meetings as able, coordinating coverage with the Scoutmaster
  \item Attend campouts and events as able, providing two-deep leadership coverage
  \item Be familiar with and follow Troop 413 Policies \& Procedures
  \item Attend quarterly Courts of Honor in the Field Uniform
  \item Model the Scout Oath and Law in all interactions
  \item Coordinate with outgoing role-holder during transition
\end{itemize}
