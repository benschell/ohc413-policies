This is a two-part checklist covering what's needed to bring on a new registered adult leader, regardless of role. Part 1 is handled by the unit; Part 2 is completed by the new leader. For what a specific role does and its own training requirements, see that role's page in the \textit{Troop 413 Adult Leadership Roles \& Responsibilities} guide.

\section{Part 1: Unit Responsibilities}

\textbf{\color{scoutgreen} Before BSA/Council Approval}
\begin{checklist}
  \item Confirm the applicant has completed current Safeguarding Youth Training (SYT) before accepting their application
  \item Help the new leader complete the BSA Adult Application and background check
  \item Submit the application to the Chartered Organization (King Moravian Church) for approval
\end{checklist}

\textbf{\color{scoutgreen} After BSA/Council Approval} (do not grant any of the below until registration is fully approved)
\begin{checklist}
  \item Set up the new leader's troop Google Workspace account
  \item Add the new leader to the appropriate Google Chat groups
  \item Help the new leader install Google Workspace and Chat on their phone
  \item Grant Scoutbook access appropriate to their role, per Troop 413 Policies \& Procedures
  \item Assign an outgoing role-holder or mentor for a transition briefing
  \item Introduce the new leader to the Scouts, families, and committee
\end{checklist}

\section{Part 2: New Leader Responsibilities}

\textbf{\color{scoutgreen} Before BSA/Council Approval}
\begin{checklist}
  \item Set up your my.scouting.org account (required to take SYT)
  \item Complete current Safeguarding Youth Training (SYT) before submitting your application
  \item Complete the BSA Adult Application and background check
\end{checklist}

\textbf{\color{scoutgreen} After BSA/Council Approval}
\begin{checklist}
  \item Complete the position-specific training listed on your role's page in the Adult Leadership Guide
  \item \textit{Program Leadership only:} complete IOLS and Hazardous Weather Training
  \item Install Google Workspace and Chat on your phone
  \item Read your role's page in the Adult Leadership Guide
  \item Meet with the outgoing role-holder (if any) and the Committee Chair or Scoutmaster
  \item Obtain a Field Uniform
  \item Attend your first troop committee meeting or troop meeting
\end{checklist}
