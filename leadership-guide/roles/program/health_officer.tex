\section{Role Overview}
The Health Officer manages the troop's medical readiness for outings: collecting and securing medical forms, overseeing medication collection before campouts, and administering or supervising medication during camping events. Medical records are handled by the Health Officer only, not by youth leadership.

\vspace{0.4em}
\section{Responsibilities}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Collect and organize BSA Annual Health and Medical Records directly from Scouts and families before each outing
  \item Ensure the correct forms are on hand for each outing based on its length and activities
  \item Oversee the collection of medications from Scouts before campouts
  \item Administer or supervise administration of medication during camping events per troop policy
  \item Maintain a secure, confidential system for medical forms and medication logs
  \item Coordinate with the Scoutmaster on medical concerns or accommodations for an outing
\end{itemize}

\section{Training Requirements}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Required:} Current Safeguarding Youth Training (SYT)
  \item \textbf{Recommended:} Wilderness First Aid or equivalent medical/EMT background
\end{itemize}

\section{Notes \& Resources}
Keep medical forms and medication logs in a secure, confidential location, following Guide to Safe Scouting guidance on health forms and medication administration. The outgoing Health Officer should hand off current medical forms, medication logs, and any ongoing medical accommodations during transition. This role is typically filled by an Assistant Scoutmaster, given the outing attendance it requires.
