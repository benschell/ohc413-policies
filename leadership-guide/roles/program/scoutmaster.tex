\section{Role Overview}
The Scoutmaster leads the troop's program in partnership with the youth-led Patrol Leaders' Council, training and mentoring the Senior Patrol Leader and other youth leaders. The committee handles resources and administration so the Scoutmaster can focus on delivering the program.

\vspace{0.3em}
\section{Responsibilities}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Deliver the Scouting program through troop meetings, outings, and events
  \item Train and mentor the Senior Patrol Leader and other youth leaders
  \item Attend and coach the Patrol Leaders' Council at its monthly planning meeting
  \item Ensure Youth Protection and safety guidelines are followed at all troop activities
  \item Conduct Scoutmaster Conferences for rank advancement
  \item Recruit and coordinate Assistant Scoutmasters
  \item Represent the troop's program needs to the committee
\end{itemize}

\section{Training Requirements}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Required:} Current Safeguarding Youth Training (SYT)
  \item \textbf{Required:} Scoutmaster/Assistant Scoutmaster Leader Specific Training (my.scouting.org)
  \item \textbf{Required:} Introduction to Outdoor Leader Skills (IOLS)
  \item \textbf{Required:} Hazardous Weather Training (my.scouting.org)
  \item \textbf{Recommended:} Wood Badge
\end{itemize}

\section{Expectations}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Attend weekly troop meetings
  \item Attend the monthly Patrol Leaders' Council meeting
  \item Attend Troop Committee meetings to present a program report and seek guidance
  \item Attend campouts and events as able, arranging ASM coverage when unavailable
  \item Be familiar with and follow Troop 413 Policies \& Procedures
  \item Attend quarterly Courts of Honor in the Field Uniform
  \item Model the Scout Oath and Law in all interactions
  \item Coordinate with outgoing role-holder during transition
\end{itemize}
