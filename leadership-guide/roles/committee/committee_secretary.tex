\section{Role Overview}
The Secretary keeps the committee organized and accountable by recording meeting minutes, maintaining committee records, and handling correspondence. This role ensures decisions and action items are documented so the committee can track follow-through over time.

\vspace{0.4em}
\section{Responsibilities}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Record minutes at each committee meeting, including decisions and action items
  \item Distribute meeting minutes to the committee in a timely manner
  \item Maintain an organized archive of past minutes and committee records
  \item Maintain the committee roster and contact information
  \item Handle troop correspondence (thank-you notes, official letters)
  \item Assist the Committee Chair with agenda preparation
  \item Keep the troop calendar on the website up to date
  \item Track photo release preferences and notify leaders of Scouts without consent on file
\end{itemize}

\section{Training Requirements}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Required:} Current Safeguarding Youth Training (SYT)
  \item \textbf{Required:} Scouts BSA - Troop Committee Position-Specific Training (my.scouting.org)
\end{itemize}

\section{Notes \& Resources}
Keep minutes and committee records in a shared folder accessible to the full committee. Key BSA resource: \textit{Troop Committee Guidebook} (BSA \#34505). The outgoing Secretary should hand off the minutes archive, committee roster, and any standing correspondence templates during transition. This is a required registered committee position; it cannot be filled by program leadership or an unregistered volunteer.
