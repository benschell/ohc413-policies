\section{Role Overview}
The Chartered Organization Representative (COR) is King Moravian Church's appointed liaison to Troop 413, holding final authority over the unit. Unlike other committee roles, the COR is appointed directly by the chartering organization, not elected or hired by the committee.

\vspace{0.4em}
\section{Responsibilities}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Serve as the liaison between King Moravian Church and Troop 413
  \item Review and approve adult leader applications and registrations before individuals may begin serving
  \item Represent Troop 413 at Old Hickory Council as the chartering organization's voting member
  \item Appoint or approve the Committee Chair and Scoutmaster on behalf of the chartering organization
  \item Resolve disputes or concerns escalated beyond the Troop Committee
  \item Ensure the unit's program aligns with the chartering organization's mission and values
\end{itemize}

\section{Training Requirements}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Required:} Current Safeguarding Youth Training (SYT)
\end{itemize}

\section{Expectations}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Approve adult leader applications and registrations in a timely manner
  \item Attend committee meetings as needed to stay informed and provide guidance
  \item Be familiar with and follow Troop 413 Policies \& Procedures
  \item Attend quarterly Courts of Honor in the Field Uniform
  \item Model Scout values in all interactions
  \item Coordinate with an incoming representative during any transition
\end{itemize}
