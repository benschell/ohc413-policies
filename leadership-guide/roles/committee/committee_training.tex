\section{Role Overview}
The Training Coordinator tracks adult leader training compliance, promotes upcoming council training opportunities to adults and youth, and ensures Introduction to Leadership Skills for Troops (ILST) is held for newly elected youth leaders after every election.

\vspace{0.4em}
\section{Responsibilities}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Track adult leader training records and follow up on expired or missing requirements
  \item Promote upcoming council and district training opportunities (Wood Badge, NYLT, etc.) to adults and youth
  \item Schedule and coordinate Introduction to Leadership Skills for Troops (ILST) after every youth leadership election
  \item Coordinate with the Scoutmaster on youth leadership training needs
  \item Maintain a troop training tracker shared with the Committee Chair
  \item Help onboard new adult leaders with required initial trainings
\end{itemize}

\section{Training Requirements}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Required:} Current Safeguarding Youth Training (SYT)
  \item \textbf{Required:} Scouts BSA - Troop Committee Position-Specific Training (my.scouting.org)
  \item \textbf{Recommended:} Wood Badge
\end{itemize}

\section{Notes \& Resources}
ILST curriculum and the council training calendar are available through the district/council training committee. The outgoing Training Coordinator should hand off the adult training tracker, the upcoming ILST schedule, and any pending training follow-ups during transition. This is a functional role: a registered committee member is preferred, but it may be filled by program leadership if needed.
