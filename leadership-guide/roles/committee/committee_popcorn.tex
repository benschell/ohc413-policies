\section{Role Overview}
The Popcorn Kernel runs the troop's Fall popcorn sale, coordinating orders, storefront sales, and prizes with the council, Secretary, and Treasurer so proceeds go toward Scout accounts.

\vspace{0.4em}
\section{Responsibilities}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Order popcorn from the council based on projected troop participation
  \item Manage popcorn inventory, tracking stock on hand and distribution to Scouts
  \item Schedule storefront sale locations and times, and coordinate with the Secretary to get dates on the troop calendar
  \item Coordinate with the Treasurer to ensure change and card payment processing are available at storefront sales
  \item Promote the sale, including online sales and prize incentives, to families
  \item Track individual Scout sales and coordinate with the Treasurer on prize qualification and Scout account credit
  \item Issue popcorn to Scouts per Troop 413 Policies \& Procedures
  \item Return unsold popcorn and settle final payment with the council by the deadline
\end{itemize}

\section{Training Requirements}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Required:} Current Safeguarding Youth Training (SYT)
  \item \textbf{Recommended:} Attend the council's popcorn kickoff/training session
\end{itemize}

\section{Notes \& Resources}
Popcorn program details, prize structure, and ordering deadlines are published by the council each year. The outgoing Kernel should hand off the council contact, current popcorn inventory count, and the per-Scout sales tracking sheet during transition. This is a functional role: a registered committee member is preferred, but it may be filled by program leadership if needed.
