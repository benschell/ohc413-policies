\section{Role Overview}
The Outdoor/Activities Chair handles the logistics behind the troop's outdoor program: campsite reservations, safety approvals, and transportation. This role lets the Scoutmaster and youth leaders focus on running the outing instead of arranging it.

\vspace{0.4em}
\section{Responsibilities}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Plan the troop's annual outdoor program with the Scoutmaster and give outing dates to the Secretary for the troop calendar
  \item Reserve campsites and secure permits for campouts and high-adventure trips
  \item Ensure outings follow Guide to Safe Scouting guidelines and any required council approvals
  \item Arrange transportation and coordinate drivers for outings
  \item Coordinate equipment needs with the Quartermaster
  \item Track outing headcounts and permission slips
  \item Support the annual program planning conference
\end{itemize}

\section{Training Requirements}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Required:} Current Safeguarding Youth Training (SYT)
  \item \textbf{Required:} Scouts BSA - Troop Committee Position-Specific Training (my.scouting.org)
\end{itemize}

\section{Notes \& Resources}
Keep campsite contacts and reservation details in a shared location accessible to the Scoutmaster; outing dates belong on the troop's shared calendar, maintained by the Secretary. Key BSA resource: \textit{Guide to Safe Scouting}. The outgoing Outdoor/Activities Chair should hand off campsite contacts and any standing reservations during transition. This is a functional role: a registered committee member is preferred, but it may be filled by program leadership if needed.
