\section{Role Overview}
The Summer Camp Coordinator manages the troop's summer camp registration and logistics each year, from camp selection through medical forms, payments, and merit badge sign-ups, so Scouts and families have a smooth path to camp.

\vspace{0.4em}
\section{Responsibilities}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Research and recommend summer camp options to the Scoutmaster and PLC each year
  \item Handle camp registration, deposits, and payment deadlines for the troop
  \item Track individual Scout sign-ups and coordinate with the Treasurer on Scout account payments
  \item Collect and verify BSA Annual Health and Medical Records for camp attendees
  \item Coordinate merit badge and program sign-ups with the Scoutmaster and Advancement Coordinator
  \item Communicate camp deadlines, packing lists, and requirements to families
  \item Coordinate adult leader coverage and registration for camp week
\end{itemize}

\section{Training Requirements}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Required:} Current Safeguarding Youth Training (SYT)
  \item \textbf{Required:} Scouts BSA - Troop Committee Position-Specific Training (my.scouting.org)
\end{itemize}

\section{Notes \& Resources}
Camp details, deadlines, and required forms vary by council and camp each year. The outgoing Coordinator should hand off camp contacts, the current registration status, and the medical form tracking sheet during transition. This is a functional role: a registered committee member is preferred, but it may be filled by program leadership if needed.
