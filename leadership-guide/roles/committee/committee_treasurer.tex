\section{Role Overview}
The Treasurer manages the troop's finances, keeping accurate records of the bank account, individual Scout accounts, and event payments. This role gives the committee, parents, and Scouts confidence that troop funds are tracked and spent responsibly.

\vspace{0.4em}
\section{Responsibilities}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Maintain accurate financial records, reconciled monthly and kept current for the annual charter renewal
  \item Train and oversee the Troop Scribe in collecting dues and forms from patrol leaders at troop meetings
  \item Track individual Scout accounts, including dues, fundraising credit, and event/campout payments
  \item Publish a monthly Scout Account Statement to parents
  \item Present a monthly financial report to the committee: Balance Sheet, Statement of Activities, and Budget vs. Actual
  \item Process reimbursements and verify receipts
  \item Prepare the annual budget with the Committee Chair
\end{itemize}

\section{Training Requirements}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Required:} Current Safeguarding Youth Training (SYT)
  \item \textbf{Required:} Scouts BSA - Troop Committee Position-Specific Training (my.scouting.org)
\end{itemize}

\section{Notes \& Resources}
Maintain the Scout account ledger and share view access with the Committee Chair. Key BSA resource: \textit{Troop Committee Guidebook} (BSA \#34505). The outgoing Treasurer should hand off bank and account access, the current ledger, and the most recent reconciled statement during transition. This is a required registered committee position; it cannot be filled by program leadership or an unregistered volunteer.
