## Proposed Policy for Review — Uniform Policy

*Draft for committee review on 9/2. If approved, this section will be added to the Troop 413 Policies document.*

Scouting America national policy defines a full Field Uniform down to the shirt, pants, neckerchief, socks, belt, and hat — all sourced from Scouting America. Troop 413 sets its own, more flexible standard for both required uniform types.

### Field Uniform

For the Field Uniform, worn at Courts of Honor, Boards of Review, while traveling, and other formal occasions, Scouts are expected to wear:

- The official Scouting America Field Uniform shirt
- The Troop-approved neckerchief
- Olive green bottoms (pants, shorts, or skirt) — any brand is acceptable, provided the color and style are consistent with the standard Scouting America uniform pant

All other components of the Field Uniform (hat, belt, socks, sash, etc.) are optional and left to individual/family discretion.

### Activity Uniform

For the Activity Uniform, worn at regular Troop meetings and other informal activities, Scouts are expected to wear:

- Any Scouting-related t-shirt (Troop 413, other unit, camp, or Scouting America event shirt)
- Olive green bottoms (pants, shorts, or skirt), as described above
