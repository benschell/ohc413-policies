## Introduction

Pack 413 is a Family Scouting Unit of the Old Hickory Council, Scouting America, and is sponsored by King Moravian Church, located at 234 West Dalton Road, King NC, 27021.

Our Pack number "413" alludes to the passage of scripture found in Philippians 4:13 – "I can do all things through Christ who strengthens me". The number 13 also has significance in Moravian Church history: the first Moravian Lovefeast, held on August 13, 1727, and the November 13 commemoration of the Chief Elder Festival, dating back to 1741. Each of these dates are commemorated annually with a Lovefeast or Communion service in Moravian Churches.

The following policies are approved by the Pack Committee and with the oversight of the King Moravian Church Joint Boards. They are intended to guide Pack operations, with a goal of setting clear policies for members, leaders, and families to follow in the structure, administration, and programming of Scouting America Pack 413. While our Pack welcomes all families regardless of faith or creed, these policies are set (and at times amended) under the long-held Moravian guiding principle: "In essentials unity, in non-essentials liberty, in all things love."

## Members of the Committee

A Pack Committee Member is defined as any adult volunteer who is officially registered with Scouting America in the role of Pack Committee Member and is current on all required training (including position-specific training and Safeguarding Youth policies). All registered Committee Members are invited to attend and provide input during Committee meetings.

---

## Active Committee Members

Official Committee meeting minutes, agendas, and sensitive business documents shall be distributed exclusively to Active Committee Members to ensure focused communication and appropriate handling of committee business.

An individual is considered an Active Committee Member if they meet one of the following criteria:

- They currently hold a named, designated Pack Committee officer role (e.g., Committee Chair, Treasurer, Secretary, Advancement Chair).
- They were recorded as present during the meeting for which the documents are being distributed.
- They have attended two out of the last four of the Committee's regularly scheduled meetings, demonstrating sustained engagement.

---

## Committee Operating Structure

The Committee shall maintain a structured operating style to ensure clear accountability, transparency, and efficient management of all Pack activities and resources.

- **Quorum:** Quorum, necessary to commence or conduct business, requires a simple majority (more than fifty percent) of all active, eligible Committee members to be present.
- **Voting Standard:** All motions, decisions, and approvals are determined by a simple majority vote of the members present, provided that quorum is met.
- **Key Approvals:** Approval of all meeting minutes, financial expenditures, dispute resolutions, and policy decisions shall be secured by a simple majority vote of members present after quorum has been established.

---

## Committee Term Limits

The Committee establishes specific terms for adult volunteer roles to ensure stability and routine leadership refreshment. A Program Year is defined as July 1 through June 30.

- **Committee Chairperson:** The Chair is appointed to a one program year term and serves at the pleasure of the Chartering Organization. The Chair may be considered for additional one-year terms upon a motion and simple majority vote of the Committee, pending final approval from the Chartering Organization.
- **General Committee Roles (e.g., Treasurer, Secretary, Advancement Chair):** All general Committee members volunteering for these roles are expected to commit to a one program year term. Members are encouraged to serve continuous terms, renewing their commitment annually with the Pack Committee at the start of each Program Year.

## Money Handling

The purpose of this policy is to establish clear guidelines and procedures for the handling of all financial assets of Pack 413, ensuring transparency, accountability, and the protection of the unit's funds.

### 1. Authorized Signers

The Pack 413 bank account shall have a minimum of three (3) and a maximum of four (4) authorized active registered adult signers at all times. Changes to authorized signers require approval by the Pack Committee and must be promptly updated with the financial institution. Spouses/partners of the Treasurer shall not be an authorized signer.

### 2. Check Writing and Disbursements

- All checks written on behalf of Pack 413 require two (2) authorized signatures.
- One of the required signatures must be that of the Treasurer. In the event the Treasurer is unavailable, the Committee Chair or an authorized signer they designate may approve and provide one of the two required signatures.
- No blank checks shall be signed. All checks must be fully completed (payee, amount, date) before signatures are affixed.
- All disbursements must be supported by appropriate documentation (e.g., invoices, receipts, reimbursement requests, vendor statements). This documentation must be submitted to the Treasurer for record-keeping.

### 3. Cash Handling and Deposits

- All cash and checks received by the unit (e.g., from fundraisers, event registrations, dues) shall be counted by at least two (2) unrelated adult volunteers immediately upon receipt, whenever practical.
- A receipt should be issued for all cash transactions. For events with multiple transactions, a log should be maintained detailing cash received.
- All funds (cash and checks) must be deposited into the Pack 413 bank account in a timely manner, generally within three (3) business days of receipt, or as soon as the amount exceeds $100, whichever comes first.
- Funds should never be held personally for extended periods.
- Deposit slips should be reconciled with the initial count and retained for record-keeping.

### 4. Expense Reimbursement

- All expenses must be pre-approved by the Treasurer or Committee Chair before the expense is incurred. Only pre-approved expenses will be reimbursed. If the Treasurer is the individual seeking reimbursement, the request must be approved by the Committee Chair.
- Individuals incurring approved expenses on behalf of the pack must submit a reimbursement request along with original receipts or detailed invoices to the Treasurer.
- Reimbursement requests should be submitted within 30 days of the expense being incurred.

### 5. Record Keeping and Reconciliation

- The Treasurer is responsible for maintaining accurate and up-to-date financial records for all income and expenditures using the unit's designated accounting software.
- Bank statements shall be reconciled in a timely manner by the Treasurer.
- A second authorized signer (not the Treasurer) should review the monthly bank reconciliation and initial it to confirm their review.
- All financial records, including bank statements, cancelled checks, deposit slips, receipts, and invoices, shall be retained in an organized manner for a minimum of seven (7) years, or as required by applicable tax laws and Scouting America policies.

### 6. Financial Reporting

- The Treasurer shall provide a financial report to the Pack Committee at each regular committee meeting, detailing income, expenditures, and current account balances.
- An annual financial report shall be presented to the Pack Committee and made available to all parents/guardians.

## Camping Requirements

Cub Scouts is a family-centric program. In line with the Scouting America "Guide for Safe Scouting", Pack 413 requires all Scouts to be accompanied by a parent or legal guardian. Exceptions to this policy may be granted on a case-by-case basis only by an approved motion by the Pack Committee.

## Leadership Expectations

The adult leaders of Pack 413 are expected to be role models by living the principles of the Scout Oath and Scout Law, and demonstrating these values through their actions toward Scouts, fellow leaders, and parents. Leaders must always strive to be approachable, encouraging, positive, and constructive in their interactions.

### Commitment to Conduct and Accountability

All adult leaders agree to comply with the Scouter Code of Conduct (linked here: https://www.scouting.org/health-and-safety/guidelines-policies), as stated on the Scouting America Adult Application. Leaders are accountable for their conduct and for modeling appropriate behavior in all Pack activities.

### Reporting Concerns

Concerns about a leader's conduct should first be resolved by bringing those concerns to the Pack Committee. Serious concerns regarding Safeguarding Youth violations must be reported immediately to the appropriate authorities and the Scouting America contact.

### Final Authority

The chartering organization, King Moravian Church, holds the final authority for the Pack. The chartering organization must officially approve all adult leader applications and registrations before the individual may begin serving in a leadership capacity.

## Program Leadership Role Definitions

Pack 413 has the following Program Leadership roles:

**Cubmaster:** Provides overall leadership and direction for the Pack's youth program, acting as the primary public face and chief communicator of the program to both the Scouts and their families. This individual is responsible for being current on all required Scouting America training, and works closely with the Pack Committee to develop the annual program plan and secure necessary resources. Serving as the Master of Ceremonies for all Pack meetings, they ensure the timely presentation of advancement awards and serve as the primary coach and resource for the Den Leaders, ensuring consistent program delivery. The Cubmaster is requested to attend all Pack Committee meetings to present a program report, seek guidance, and act as the executive liaison, serving at the pleasure of the Committee.

**Assistant Cubmaster:** Supports the Cubmaster in all duties, proactively accepts and manages ongoing program responsibilities delegated by the Cubmaster, and assists in mentoring and supporting the Den Leaders. This individual is the primary backup, ensuring seamless program continuity and leadership succession, and fills the role of the Cubmaster in their absence for Scout-facing meetings and activities. The Assistant Cubmaster serves at the pleasure of the Pack Committee.

**Den Leaders (Multiple, Based on Need):** Conducts regular Den meetings for one or more specific ranks/ages of Scouts, and is responsible for adhering to and enforcing Safeguarding Youth policies and safety standards. They are responsible for planning, preparing, and executing the Cub Scout adventures necessary for rank advancement, and actively recruit and supervise parent volunteers to assist with meeting execution and logistics. The Den Leader serves as the primary communication link between the Den's families and the Pack leadership, serving at the pleasure of the Pack Committee.

---

## Program Leadership Term Limits

The Committee establishes term limits for key Program Leadership roles to ensure the sustainable rotation of volunteers and leadership continuity. A Program Year is defined as July 1 through June 30.

- **Cubmaster:** The expected service commitment for the Cubmaster role is a one program year term, having served as the Assistant Cubmaster for the prior year or having equivalent prior experience. Cubmasters are welcome to serve for additional one program year terms as desired, with agreement by the Pack Committee.
- **Assistant Cubmaster:** The expected service commitment for the Assistant Cubmaster role is a single one-year term, immediately preceding service as Cubmaster. This role is intended as a apprenticeship for the subsequent Cubmaster term.
- **Den Leaders:** Den Leaders are expected to serve for the full duration of a given program year. Den Leaders shall confirm their commitment annually.
- **Succession Planning:** The Committee Chairperson and the current Cubmaster shall actively identify, recruit, and mentor the incoming Assistant Cubmaster one year in advance of the Cubmaster transition.

---

## Visitor Policy

Pack 413 is an open organization that encourages prospective Scouts and their families to experience our program. Visitors are welcome to attend Pack or Den meetings up to a maximum of two (2) meetings or Pack activities for the purpose of trying out the program.

All visiting youth and their accompanying parent/guardian must check-in upon arrival and submit a signed Visitor Waiver of Liability and Emergency Contact Form prior to the start of the activity. Visitors are expected to adhere to all Pack rules and safety guidelines while participating. After the second visit, registration materials must be submitted to continue participation.
